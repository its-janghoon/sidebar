# Sidebar privacy policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Sidebar is an Atlassian Forge app made by Janghoon Lee. This page says what it does with your
data.

## What Sidebar reads

- Contract files (`.docx`, `.txt`, `.md`) attached to Jira issues where you open the Sidebar panel.
- The Confluence page titled `Sidebar Playbook`.
- Issue fields, comments and earlier requests with the same counterparty, to answer questions
  about past matters.

## What Sidebar stores

Contract versions, review results and the decisions you record (accept, reply) are kept in
Forge storage on your Atlassian site. Nothing is stored anywhere else.

## Where data goes

- Sidebar runs entirely on Atlassian (Forge functions, Forge storage, Forge LLM). It sends no
  data to servers outside Atlassian, and it has no analytics or tracking.
- Before contract text goes to Forge LLM, party names, emails, phone numbers, bank numbers and
  amounts are replaced with placeholders.
- The developer cannot see your contracts, reviews or decisions.

## Deleting data

Uninstalling Sidebar removes its Forge storage from your site, following Atlassian's Forge data
rules.
