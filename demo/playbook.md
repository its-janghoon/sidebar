## Limitation of liability

Match: liability, limitation of liability, liability cap

Risk: high

Standard: Each party's total liability is capped at the fees paid or payable in the 12 months before the claim.

Allowed: A cap between 12 and 24 months of fees, applying to both parties.

Fallback: Each party's total cumulative liability for all claims arising out of or relating to this Agreement will not be more than the fees paid or payable by Customer in the 12 months before the claim.

Red flags: 1 month; unlimited; uncapped

## Indirect damages

Match: indirect damages, consequential, indirect damages waiver

Risk: high

Standard: The waiver of lost profits and indirect damages is mutual and protects both parties.

Allowed: Carve-outs for confidentiality breaches and indemnity obligations, if they apply to both parties.

Fallback: Except for a breach of Section 10 (Confidentiality), under no circumstances will either party be liable to the other for lost profits or revenues, or for consequential, special, indirect, exemplary, punitive, or incidental damages.

Red flags: will provider be liable to customer; will customer be liable to provider

## Governing law

Match: governing law, chosen courts, jurisdiction

Risk: medium

Standard: New York law and the state or federal courts in New York County.

Allowed: New York, California, or England and Wales.

Fallback: The laws of the State of New York will govern all interpretations and disputes about this Agreement, and the parties will bring any suit in the state or federal courts in New York County.

Red flags: delaware

## Payment disputes

Match: payment dispute, payment, fees

Risk: low

Standard: Customer has at least 30 days after an automatic payment to dispute fees.

Allowed: 30 to 60 days.

Fallback: Customer must notify Provider about the dispute before payment is due, or within 30 days of an automatic payment.

Red flags: within 10 days; within 7 days; within 5 days

## Renewal and termination

Match: term, termination, renewal

Risk: low

Standard: Automatic renewal is fine if either party can give notice of non-renewal at least 30 days before the end of the term.

Allowed: Notice periods of 30 to 90 days.

Fallback: The Agreement renews for additional Subscription Periods unless one party gives notice of non-renewal at least 30 days before the end of the current period.

Red flags: no right to terminate; 180 days
